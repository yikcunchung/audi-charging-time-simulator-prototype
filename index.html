  1. 1

    The charging time of the battery and the maximum DC charging power at HPC charging stations (High Power Charging) were determined in accordance with DIN 70080 and may vary depending on various factors, such as the ambient and battery temperature, the use of other country-specific connectors, the use of interior climate control (e.g. remote climate control of the vehicle), the pre-conditioning function used to bring the traction battery to a temperature suitable for charging/driving, the power availability at the charging station, the state of charge and battery ageing. The charging power decreases as the state of charge increases. Charging losses are taken into account. The charging power depends on the type of mains connector used, the country-specific voltage, the number of phases available in the power grid and the customer-specific home installation. It may therefore be lower in individual cases. The mains charging cable with industrial plug and the operating unit of the charging system are matched to each other and enable a specific charging power. A later change of the mains charging cable to a different industrial plug is technically possible, but may result in a lower charging power.
